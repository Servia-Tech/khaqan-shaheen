Khaqan Shaheen: CV and career history

Khaqan Shaheen has worked in web and business systems since 2008 and has been Head of IT of Aalmir / MIR Plastic Industries in Sharjah and Dubai since December 2015, where he owns the entire IT function for a plastics manufacturing group across six sites in five countries, around 150 users, a team of six plus outsourced developers, reporting directly to the owner. Before that: Web Projects Manager and Webmaster for The City School Group in Lahore, Webmaster and Project Manager at Infobyte Solutions, and web and design delivery at All In One Technology in Abu Dhabi. Bachelor of Arts from the University of the Punjab, a PMP course in Abu Dhabi, and four CXO DX awards between 2023 and 2024. Based in Dubai, open to senior roles and to relocation.

Career history

  1. December 2015 to present. Head of IT, Aalmir / MIR Plastic Industries LLC, Sharjah and Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Owns the entire IT function for a plastics manufacturing group: six sites in five countries, around 150 users, a team of six plus outsourced developers, reporting directly to the owner. One Odoo ERP implemented and owned across production, sales, inventory, procurement, accounting, HR and maintenance. PostgreSQL 9.5 to 16 across eight major versions with no unplanned downtime. Five AI systems in daily production. Networks, firewalls, site to site VPNs, identity, telephony, CCTV and access control. Vendor selection, negotiation and IT purchasing. The full IT setup from nothing whenever the group opens a new factory. The group's four brand websites and their search and paid marketing.
  2. July 2012 to December 2015. Web Projects Manager and Webmaster, The City School Group Head Office, Lahore, Pakistan. Planned and delivered web projects and managed the web presence and social media for a national school network.
  3. January 2012 to August 2012. Webmaster and Project Manager, Infobyte Solutions, Pakistan. Web delivery and project management for client work.
  4. August 2009 to 2011. Web and design delivery, All In One Technology, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.
  5. 2008 to 2009. Web and graphic design roles, Lahore, Pakistan. Started as a web design intern in July 2008, then design and web roles at Lahore studios before moving to the UAE.
  6. Alongside the day job: a small technology venture of his own, built end to end. The web application, the booking flow, the mobile and Wear OS watch apps, the customer messaging automation, the search and content systems, the analytics and the paid search. Most of the AI and automation work now running in the day job was built and broken there first.

What the current role actually covers

ERP: one Odoo instance for six sites in five countries, with custom development specified and kept upgrade-safe, 256 models, 1,187 custom fields and 686 access rules. Infrastructure: Ubuntu and Nginx on-premise and across AWS, Google Cloud and DigitalOcean, PostgreSQL through eight major versions, backups, point in time recovery, off-site replication and monitoring. Security and identity: Google Workspace single sign-on, two factor authentication, role based access, firewalls, site to site VPNs and endpoint protection across five countries. AI: document OCR into the ERP, automated document verification, a WhatsApp and web sales agent, a voice AI agent on Grandstream telephony, and one multi-channel chat platform, all in daily production. Devices: biometric attendance, geo-verified patrol, IP CCTV, access control and machine records integrated with the ERP. Web and marketing: four brand websites with their SEO, Google Ads and GA4 reporting.
