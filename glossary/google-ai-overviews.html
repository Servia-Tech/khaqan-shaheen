What is AI Overviews (Google)?

AI Overviews are the AI-generated summaries Google shows at the top of some search results, built by its Gemini models from pages in Google's index, with links to the sources it used; they launched in 2024 and now appear for many queries.

AI Overviews grew out of Google's Search Generative Experience, which ran in Search Labs during 2023, and became a standard part of results in the United States in May 2024 before spreading to well over a hundred countries. When Google judges that a query benefits from a synthesised answer, it generates a short summary with a Gemini model and shows it above the organic results, with a panel of source links. A separate AI Mode offers a fuller conversational version of the same idea.

For a business this changes what a click is worth. If the overview answers the question, many people never scroll to the links, so the goal shifts from ranking to being one of the cited sources. Eligibility works like ordinary search: if Googlebot can crawl and index a page, and the page is not marked noindex or nosnippet, it can be used. The Google-Extended robots token controls Gemini training and grounding in other products and has no effect on Search or on AI Overviews, a point misunderstood often enough to be worth repeating.

An example: a company that installs CCTV writes a page answering what the local compliance rules require, with the steps as a numbered list, a named author and a last-updated date. That page is exactly the kind of thing an overview quotes, because it is specific, structured and attributable.

The common mistake is chasing overviews with thin, generic content. Google's own guidance is that no special optimisation exists; the pages it cites are the ones that would have ranked anyway, written clearly enough to be summarised without distortion.

Related terms

  • AI search visibility: AI search visibility is how often, and how accurately, a website is used and cited by AI-driven search products such as ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot and Claude when people ask questions the site could answer.
  • AEO (answer engine optimisation): Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is the practice of structuring web content so that answer engines, meaning AI assistants, voice assistants and featured snippets, can extract a direct, accurate answer to a question and attribute it to your site.
  • robots.txt for AI crawlers: robots.txt for AI crawlers means adding User-agent rules to the standard robots.txt file at a site's root that allow or disallow named AI bots individually, so a site can, for example, refuse model training while still allowing AI search engines to index it.
  • JSON-LD structured data: JSON-LD structured data is a block of JSON, placed in a script tag with type application/ld+json, that describes a page's content using schema.org vocabulary (organisation, article, product, FAQ) so search engines and AI systems can read the facts without parsing the visible HTML.
